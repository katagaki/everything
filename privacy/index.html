Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 6 October 2026

(This privacy policy supersedes PRIVACY.md and Privacy Policy.pdf.)

Data Collection

Except as described in Logging below, my apps do not collect, store, or process any personal data or information from you. No cookies, analytics, tracking tools, or third-party services are used to gather information about you. Except as described below, any data required to operate my apps are stored on-device.

Some of my apps may send data to servers hosted on Cloudflare for processing. These servers may store an App Attest ID (used to verify that requests come from a genuine copy of my app) or usage limits data (such as the number of requests made), but no personal data is stored, except as described in Logging below.

Logging

To monitor errors and keep my servers running reliably, my servers hosted on Cloudflare keep logs of the requests they receive. These logs are generated automatically by Cloudflare and may include request details (such as the URL, method, headers, and time of the request), your IP address, approximate location derived from it (such as country or city), information about your network connection, and response details (such as status codes and processing time). They may also include error messages generated by my servers. The contents of your requests and the responses sent back to you are never logged.

These logs are only used for debugging and maintaining my servers. They are not used to identify, track, or profile you, are not shared with anyone other than Cloudflare, and are automatically deleted after a maximum of 7 days.

Third-Party Services

When you download or use my apps through the App Store, Apple may collect information as described in their privacy policy. For more information, please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

When you download or use my Android apps through Google Play, Google may collect information as described in their privacy policy. For more information, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

When my apps send data to servers hosted on Cloudflare, Cloudflare may process information as described in their privacy policy. For more information, please refer to Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Some of my apps use the Jev API by TypeSafe AI to provide AI-powered features. When you use these features, the data you provide for them may be sent to TypeSafe AI for processing, and TypeSafe AI may process this data as described in their privacy policy. For more information, please refer to TypeSafe AI's Privacy Policy.

When you login to a third-party service through my app (such as a service that is required to use my app's functionalities), that service may collect information as described in their privacy policy. For more information, please refer to the service's privacy policy on their official website. My apps will never collect and send this information to any remote server for storage.

Changes to This Policy

I may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any change will be trackable via Git commits, and the effective date will be updated.
